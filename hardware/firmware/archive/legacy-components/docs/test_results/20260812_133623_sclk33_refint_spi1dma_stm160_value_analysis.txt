Conclusion:
- Increasing STM32 from 80 to 160 MHz reduced median FSR time from 1717 us
  to 1426 us and raised complete output from 454.727 to 581.667 Hz.
- FSR acquisition alone now consumes essentially the full 1428.6 us budget
  for 700 Hz; ACC, packing and loop overhead still follow it.
- Sensor values remain concentrated at bit-boundary codes. Clocking faster
  improves throughput but does not correct the ADC conversion/readout failure.
